## 3 Core Value Cards

### Private by Default
Your full memory content stays in the database, not on-chain.

### Proof You Can Trust
We generate a canonical hash and anchor only that proof on Polygon.

### Share on Your Terms
Keep entries private, or publish a share page with verification details.

## FAQ (Landing Page)

**Is my full journal content on-chain?**  
No. Only proof metadata is anchored on-chain.

**What exactly is on-chain?**  
A memory key, content hash, owner/time metadata, and shareable state.

**Can I switch between private and shared?**  
Yes. You control visibility as the owner.
